# 6-Step Personal Mastery System - Skill Building, Strength, Deep Work & Mind Training

**Summary**: A complete personal mastery system consisting of 6 interconnected disciplines: (1) Skill Building—choose an industry based on passion (SMMA, SEO, dropshipping, copywriting, video editing), identify online opportunities, invest 3+ hours daily to master it; (2) Stop Being a Bitch—eliminate cheap dopamine (mindless consumption), replace with meaningful habits (exercise, cold showers, reading, meditation, running); (3) Master Deep Work—dedicate 3-4 hours daily to focused work, identify peak productivity hours, eliminate distractions (phone on DND), work hard when working, be done when done; (4) Train Your Mind—brain grows through training, dedicate 6+ hours weekly to skill development, use reading (think better), writing (manage better), meditation (clear better); (5) Become Strong—health goals, 50-60 min exercise 5x/week (running, calisthenics, weights), train brain (meditation, yoga), understand "motivation starts, habits sustain"; (6) Go All In—8 core principles: define goals, create plan, chase progress over perfection, keep head down, don't broadcast achievements, destroy negativity, hustle for 1 year, enjoy the journey. Core insight: everyone is focused on themselves, not you—so disappear till Dec 2024 and set yourself 3 years ahead.

Key Points:
- Skill building requires daily investment (3+ hrs) over months/years
- Eliminate cheap dopamine before adding meaningful habits
- Deep work sessions need elimination of all distractions and defined time blocks
- Brain training (reading/writing/meditation) is non-negotiable for growth
- Physical strength and mental strength are interconnected
- Discretion during growth phase (don't broadcast, keep head down)
- Work intensity must match recovery (work hard, rest fully)
- 1-year commitment with compounding returns over 3+ years
- Progress is non-linear; consistency matters more than speed
- "Motivation is what gets you started. Habit is what keeps you going."

Actionable Recommendation:
This week: Choose ONE discipline to start (skill building OR deep work OR physical training). Commit to 3-4 hours this week. Don't announce it. Just execute quietly. Next week, stack another discipline. By month 3, you'll have a system that compounds.
